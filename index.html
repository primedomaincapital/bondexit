Who would benefit most from owning BondExit.com?

Why does the exit matter so much in bonds?

Because bonds repay, mature, get called, and reprice with every move in rates and credit. A position held too long can give back income gains; a position sold carelessly can surrender yield or trigger an avoidable cost. The exit is where analysis becomes outcome, and a brand built around that discipline carries immediate authority.
